ECG quick reference for NCD patients

5-step read (about 60 seconds)
  1. Rate and rhythm: regular? P before every QRS? (AF = irregularly irregular, no P)
  2. Axis and intervals: PR 120–200 ms, QRS <120 ms, QTc <450 (M) / <460 (F) ms
  3. ST and T: elevation, depression, inversion, peaked T
  4. Q waves and R progression: pathological Q = ≥40 ms wide or >25% of the R wave
  5. Voltage and chamber: LVH (Sokolow-Lyon: S in V1 + R in V5/V6 ≥35 mm) with strain

Do an ECG: at diagnosis of T2DM or HTN (CPG baseline), chest pain or breathlessness, palpitations, syncope, K⁺ <3.0 or ≥5.5, before QT-prolonging drugs, and when starting new rate-slowing drugs. Diabetics may have silent ischaemia: no chest pain does not exclude MI.
